A few leads. All of them real.

Qualifyr finds buyer companies – not agencies, not vendors – with a named decision-maker and a verified email, and shows the evidence behind every score. It would rather give you five leads it can defend than five hundred it can't.

See how it works on GitHub →

What you get per lead

The company, classifiedBUYER / VENDOR / UNKNOWN with the exact words and pages that decided it.
A person, not a mailboxOwner, CEO or director from the company's own pages or chamber records; role mailboxes are clearly marked.
An email you can trustSyntax, MX and – where possible – a mailbox-level check. Guessed addresses are never sent to.
A score with reasons0–100 across fit, company quality, buyer evidence, contact quality and buying signals – every point explained.

Where the data comes from

OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps for discovery, the companies' own websites for evidence, chamber-of-commerce member lists for named representatives, RDAP for domain age and public news for mentions. No LinkedIn scraping, no purchased lists.

Why a human approves every email

Nothing is sent automatically. Each email is drafted from observed facts, previewed, edited if needed and approved by a person before it leaves – and a reply, bounce or "stop" ends the sequence immediately.
